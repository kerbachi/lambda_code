


 --- Functional Requirement ---

Functional requirement is an architectural view that defines what a system does by identifying the transformative processes, inputs, and outputs required to achieve mission objectives, without specifying the physical or structural implementation.  It serves as a bridge between high-level requirements and the structural design, focusing on functional decomposition to break complex operations into elementary units or workflows.

--- Non-functional requirements  ---
Non-functional requirements (NFRs) are specifications that define how a system operates, focusing on quality attributes, constraints, and performance criteria rather than specific behaviors or features.  While functional requirements describe what the system must do, NFRs dictate system-wide properties such as usability, reliability, and security, often influencing the overall system architecture and design decisions.
